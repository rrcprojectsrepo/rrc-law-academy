Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding admissions, entrance examinations, and institutional preparation.

Which courses does RRC Law Academy offer? expand_more

RRC Law Academy offers specialized preparation for undergraduate and postgraduate law entrance examinations including CLAT UG, CLAT PG, AILET UG, and AILET PG, alongside structured skill-development programs.

Who can prepare for CLAT UG? expand_more

School students in Class 11 and 12, as well as 12th pass students aspiring to secure admission into premier 5-year Integrated LL.B. programmes across National Law Universities (NLUs).

Who can prepare for CLAT PG? expand_more

Final-year LL.B. students and law graduates seeking admission into one-year LL.M. degree programmes at NLUs or preparing for recruitment into select public sector undertakings.

What is AILET? expand_more

The All India Law Entrance Test (AILET) is conducted annually by National Law University, Delhi (NLU Delhi) for admission to its undergraduate B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) and postgraduate LL.M. programmes.

What programmes are available apart from entrance courses? expand_more

RRC Law Academy provides Foundation Programs, Intensive Revision modules, Mock Test series, and Current Affairs & GK guidance, in addition to foundational legal skill development.

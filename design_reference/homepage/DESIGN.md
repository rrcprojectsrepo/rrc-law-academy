## Brand & Style

The design system projects scholarly rigor, institutional heritage, and intellectual clarity. Designed for elite aspirants of competitive law examinations (e.g., CLAT, AILET, Judicial Services) and continuous legal education, the visual posture avoids aggressive commercial ed-tech cues—such as gamified badges, cartoonish countdown meters, or neon accents—in favor of an authoritative, dignified academic atmosphere reminiscent of historic legal societies and premier law faculties.

### Design Principles
- **Scholarly Authority:** Typography, alignment, and restrained ornamentation emulate formal legal compendiums and institutional digests.
- **Calibrated Restraint:** Visual weight relies on deep navy tones, disciplined hairline gold dividers, and structured grid margins rather than heavy drop shadows or vibrant fills.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts feature generous breathing room, structured bibliographic citations, clear tabular statutes, and unambiguous hierarchy to reduce cognitive fatigue during intense legal study.
- **Sovereign Detailing:** Gold is employed strictly as an intentional editorial signal (rules, active states, key statutory distinctions) and never as decorative skeuomorphism or gloss.

## Colors

The palette balances formal sobriety with high-legibility surfaces. It rejects pure `#000000` blacks to prevent harsh contrast against soft light fields.

### Core Swatches & Token Mapping
- **Primary (`#0E1C2F`):** Deep Sovereign Navy. Applied to headers, primary action buttons, high-priority navigation bars, and primary badges.
- **Secondary (`#775A19`):** Burnished Gold. Used for authoritative accents, active navigational indicators, refined 1px divider lines, borders of premium modules, and icon fills.
- **Tertiary (`#FED488`):** Bright Warm Gold. Reserved for subtle focus rings, illuminated notification dots, callout banners, and high-priority statutory alerts.
- **Page Canvas (`#F9F9FF`):** Cool Paper White. Default body canvas providing expansive, glare-reducing reading space.
- **Light Surface (`#F0F3FF`):** Subdued Secondary Canvas. Used for nested panels, sidebars, and inactive inputs.
- **Card Surface (`#E7EEFF`):** Tinted Structural Blue. Used for content containers, syllabus modules, and course dossiers.
- **Surface Variant (`#D8E3FB`):** Border and containment tint. Used for card outlines, tab rails, and subtle divider rules.
- **Text Primary (`#111C2D`):** Deep Navy Tint. Applied to primary headings, body copy, and form values.
- **Text Muted (`#44474C`):** Chiseled Slate. Used for metadata, legal annotations, footnotes, and disabled control labels.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Base utility surface for inputs, elevated modals, and contrasting card blocks against shaded panels.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances historical legal stature with digital readability.

- **Playfair Display:** Employed strictly for display scales, major page titles, lecture milestones, and institutional credentials. It brings classical serif cadence without sacrificing legibility at medium sizes. Avoid setting italic variations except when marking formal legal citations or Latin jurisprudence terms (e.g., *ratio decidendi*, *stare decisis*).
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Governs all interface copy, curriculum outlines, data tables, case briefs, forms, and small-scale labels. It offers geometric stability, open apertures, and crisp character discrimination at small reading sizes.
- **Case Rulings & Monospace:** For statutory clause numbering (e.g., Section 302, Article 21), retain `Plus Jakarta Sans` in `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain aligned layout columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured 12-column fixed-max system (1320px maximum canvas width) with broad external margins to instill an academic journal layout.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column layout. Margin: `3rem` (`48px`). Gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`). Generous two-column academic splits (e.g., 4 columns dedicated to legal index/syllabus anchors, 8 columns dedicated to reading material and case dossiers).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margin: `2rem` (`32px`). Gutter: `1.25rem` (`20px`). Side panels collapse into top drawer rails or segmented sticky bars.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout. Margin: `1.25rem` (`20px`). Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`). Vertical stacks dominate. Editorial serifs scale automatically using the `-mobile` typographic tokens.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections within coursework use `space-xl` (`40px`) to prevent visual crowding.
- Form controls, table rows, and interactive syllabus nodes strictly abide by `space-sm` (internal element padding) and `space-md` (adjacent component separation).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurry drop shadows, high-elevation floating cards, and diffuse glow effects. Visual depth is established through a disciplined system of **tonal stepping** and **low-contrast keylines**.

### Elevation Rules
- **Flat Ground (`#F9F9FF`):** Baseline document canvas. 
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#E7EEFF` or `#FFFFFF`):** Applied to standard legal modules, course cards, and test series overviews. No box-shadow; containment is provided by a crisp `1px solid #D8E3FB` border.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Active/Hover Cards):** Maintained using a crisp dual border: `1px solid #775A19` (burnished gold) accompanied by a minimal, precise ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(14, 28, 47, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Overlays & Modals):** White `#FFFFFF` surface bordered with `1px solid #0E1C2F`, anchored over a deep navy translucent scrim (`#0E1C2F` at `65%` opacity with `4px` backdrop blur). Shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(14, 28, 47, 0.12)`.
- **Divider System:** Decorative rules use a precise `1px solid #775A19` gold hairline, often capped with an inset academic margin or center diamond anchor.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** architectural corner treatment (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius). 

### Corner Geometry
- **Inputs, Buttons, Pills, Badges:** `4px` corner radius. This conveys structural permanence, reminiscent of physical paper stock, binders, and legal diplomas.
- **Cards, Panels, Modal Sheets:** `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`). Keeps large containers structured and disciplined without drifting into soft consumer application aesthetics.
- **Tab Indicators & Badges:** Subtle pill shapes are prohibited for core navigation; all badge enclosures remain clipped rectangles with `4px` corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** `#0E1C2F` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `1px solid #0E1C2F`. Hover state shifts to `#1A2C44` with a subtle `1px solid #775A19` bottom accent line. Height: 44px (touch-compliant), padding: `0 24px`.
- **Secondary / Academic Button:** `#FFFFFF` fill, `#0E1C2F` text, `1px solid #D8E3FB`. On hover: surface transitions to `#F0F3FF` and border to `#775A19`.
- **Tertiary Link Button:** Plain text in `#775A19` with a subtle animated underline that matches the gold accent.
- *Strict Rule:* Never use pill-shaped (`50rem`) button radii or gradient fills.

### Cards
- **Dossier & Course Cards:** Background `#E7EEFF`, border `1px solid #D8E3FB`, border radius `8px`. Padding: `24px`. 
- **Header Section of Cards:** Playfair Display heading paired with a 1px burnished gold rule separating title metadata from the body brief.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips (e.g., "Prelims Focused", "Constitutional Law"):** Background `#F0F3FF`, text `#111C2D`, border `1px solid #D8E3FB`, font: `label-sm` (`uppercase`, `letterSpacing: 0.06em`).
- **Distinction Chips:** Background `#0E1C2F`, text `#FED488`, border `1px solid #775A19`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D8E3FB`, text `#111C2D`, placeholder `#44474C`. Height: 44px, border radius `4px`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `#0E1C2F` with a crisp `2px` ring in `#FED488` at 50% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Navy borders (`#0E1C2F`), checked fill in `#0E1C2F` with pure white checkmark indicator. No playful spring animations; transitions are swift and linear (150ms).

### Academic Dossier Tables & Lists
- **Legal Digest List:** Alternating item striping using `#F9F9FF` and `#F0F3FF`. Left border features a dormant `2px solid transparent` line which lights up to `2px solid #775A19` upon selection or active reading focus.
- **Statute Reference Callout:** Left-bordered callout container (`4px solid #775A19`), background `#F0F3FF`, interior typography using `body-md` with `Playfair Display` lead-ins.
